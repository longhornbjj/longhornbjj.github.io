How Do I Join?

Check out our Guide to UTBJJ Spring 2025. There you will find instructions on paying dues, submitting the verification form, and completing your membership packet. After this, join our Discord. Once you have paid your dues you will have access to all channels.

What’s Included?

  • Unlimited access to practices, socials, and competitions.
  • Sponsored competition fees.
  • Free UTBJJ T-shirt each semester, as well as access to additional merch
  • Access to our seminars

4 Free Practices, On Us

Before joining, all prospective members get to attend 4 free practices of their choice. No commitment. No sign-up. Just rolling.
